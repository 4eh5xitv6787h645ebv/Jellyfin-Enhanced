### 89, 90, 92. Reclaimerr accounts for Jellyfin users, and My requests in full

What upstream added. An administrator's button in the Reclaimerr card creates, through the service account, a Reclaimerr
account (User role, signed in by name through the trusted-proxy sign-in) for every Jellyfin user who has none, with the
permissions and pages ticked, and maps each new account to its Jellyfin user; mapped, linked and same-name users are
left alone, names under five characters get `_user`, other characters become `_`, and names Reclaimerr would refuse
are listed as not created. My requests on the Leaving soon page becomes the user's own Reclaimerr list together with what
the service account filed in their name, so a refused own list no longer hides the rest. A request filed in Reclaimerr
itself is shown as the Jellyfin user of the same name.

Here: the route is a partial of the feature's one controller (`JeMediaTagsController.Reclaimerr.cs`,
`CreateReclaimerrUsers`, `[Authorize]`, administrators only through `IsAdminUser`); upstream's hunk applies as is to
`Features/MediaTags/Services/ReclaimerrActions.cs`; the body type is in `Features/MediaTags/Models`; the page's group
is in `client/src/admin/config/sections/arr.ts` and its button in `page/service-status.ts` beside Create service account,
on the view's lifecycle. Entry 90 (upstream's bot regenerating its route inventory) is `je12 regen`'s output in the same
commit, and entry 92 (upstream's api.md row two hours later) is in it too, because the api.md docs test requires a row
for every route; the row is upstream's, the totals this tree's (227 to 228).

One difference. The configuration page also shows the Reclaimerr card as a linked copy on the Services tab. In
upstream's build that copy shows every box unticked and its button sends the *arr tab's ticks, whatever was ticked in the
copy (filed as a found bug). Here a tick in either copy is made in the other, so both show and send the same thing. The
boxes have no ids: the page's mirroring and the config-flag inventory (`docs/advanced/config-flag-groups.json`) both
treat an input with an id as a setting, and these are not settings.

against the loopback Reclaimerr: My requests with a refused own list and with both lists (fails before the port: nothing
shown, then only the own list), a request filed in Reclaimerr itself (fails before: no person), and create-users (who
may, the refusals that send nothing, which users get an account, the names, the permissions and pages sent, the

# Canopy K2 triage: guard baselines

Every (file, key) recorded in the `tests/frontend/guards/*-baseline.json` ratchets at the time of this commit, classified as follows. A key is `<rule> <enclosing function chain>[ <detail>]`, and the count is how many findings that key holds. Classification is per key.

- **likely real bug:** user-visible wrong behaviour, an XSS risk or a leak.
- **benign:** for example a trusted constant interpolated, or a page-lifetime registration.
- **unclear:** the origin or lifetime was not established without further tracing.

No fixes are included. To list findings with line numbers: `node tests/frontend/guards/record.mjs --list [guard]`.

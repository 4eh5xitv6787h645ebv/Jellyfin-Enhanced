### 119. Touch holds on the player (ported)

`client/src/features/player/long-press.ts` takes upstream's `playback.js` change: a press starts only on the playing
video surface (never a control, dialog, the up-next card, the pause screen or the Enhanced panel, never paused
playback). A touch there cancels native scrolling and is held back from jellyfin-web, which toggles the OSD on
pointerdown; a short tap is replayed on release as a touch pointerdown/pointerup, so jellyfin-web's own handler
toggles the OSD once; a hold owns the gesture (moves, release, a click within 400 ms after it). A second finger,
pointercancel, leaving the window or hiding the page cancel and restore the rate; a hold on paused playback no longer
starts it. `client/src/features/chrome/events.ts` takes upstream's `events.js` change: release and cancel reach the
player off the video page, the target exclusion moved into the player, and touchstart/touchmove are not passive.

Differences from upstream:

- The listeners upstream adds in `events.js` (pointerdown, pointermove, pointercancel, window blur, document
  visibilitychange) are added by the player's `long-press.ts` with the player lifetime's abort signal, and again after
  each lifetime reset (navigation, user switch). The leak guard's L2 ratchet admits no new global listener without a
  signal or a removal, so page-lifetime listeners in `events.ts` would have needed a raised baseline. They keep
  upstream's rules (physical input only; pointerdown and pointermove on the video page; the cancels anywhere).
- Upstream's window hashchange cancel is not added: the player lifetime already resets on navigation and its reset
  cancels the press (upstream's hashchange test passes before and after the port).
- The rewrite already installed its listeners whatever the setting was at start (CHR-EVENTS-1); upstream installs
  them only when Long press for 2x was on as the page started.

Tests: upstream's `features-long-press.test.mjs` applies unchanged at `tests/frontend/features/player/` (the harness
import one folder deeper); 11 of its 15 tests fail before the port and all pass after. A new test in
`features-player-rewrite.test.mjs` checks that after two navigations each new listener type is live once and a touch
pointerdown on the video is claimed once; it fails before the port (no listener). The two existing long-press tests
there and the one in `tests/browser/player-rewrite.spec.cjs` now press on the video element, because a press must
start on the playing video surface; the drag test marks its video as playing. Upstream's coverage-matrix sentence is
added to the Player row of `tests/docs/coverage-matrix.md`.

Lab (`p119-long-press.mjs`): the lab movie playing, Long press for 2x on for the lab admin (`je12 config set
longPress2xEnabled true --user admin`, needed on upstream's build, which installs its listeners only when the setting
is on at page start), and real touch input through Chromium's touch emulation at the middle of the video. Upstream's
build and this branch read the same:

### 44. Maintainerr actions in the item menu, and a Leaving soon home row

What upstream does: administrators mark an unlisted movie or series for removal, or keep or postpone a listed one,
from its ⋮ menu (Mark and Keep apply at once with an Undo toast; Postpone opens a chooser: +7/14/30/90 days, a custom
count, a timer reset). The item goes to the one active manual Maintainerr collection of its type on its library;
several on one library are told apart by asking each collection's Radarr or Sonarr (as Maintainerr lists them)
whether it holds the item. Four admin-only routes do the work (`GET maintainerr/item/{itemId}`,
`POST maintainerr/mark|unmark|postpone`); every change reads Maintainerr again. A Leaving soon row is registered with
Home Screen Sections when installed. The Maintainerr address, key, the two new switches and the refresh interval move
from the Media Tags page to a Maintainerr card in the *arr tab (linked copy under Services, a card in the Overview's
Service Status); the source is checked on every configuration save.

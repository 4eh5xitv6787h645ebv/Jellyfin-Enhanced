## 2. The entries

### 75. MDBList: no AniList rating source; 429 and rate-limit headers

MDBList has no AniList rating, so the `aniList` source leaves the server's source ids, the native resolver, tile
logos and colours, the Tag Styles catalogue, the web chips and pickers, the preview and the MDBList row's badge list
(and its admin "Configure ratings shown" list and sync selectors). A stored source list or Tag Styles override that
still names it is ignored, not rejected. MDBList calls pause for a 429's Retry-After (60 s when it gives none, at most
6 h), and `X-RateLimit-*` headers on any answer replace the locally counted quota. The C# hunks applied after the
moves; the client removals went into the TypeScript the hand-written scripts are built from.

Difference from upstream: `images/mdblist-logos/anilist.png` stays. The AniList review cards draw their source logo
from it (here `features/details-providers/reviews.ts`; upstream's `js/elsewhere/reviews.js` and its own reviews test
still do too), so upstream's deletion breaks that logo on its build; filed as a found bug. No cache version is bumped, as upstream: only a user whose source list named `aniList` gets a
new rating-sources digest.

### 76. GET leaving-soon/items for KefinTweaks

A standard item list (`{Items, TotalRecordCount}`) of the home row's titles (movies and series the caller can see,
soonest removal first, at most 20), cut to an optional `limit`. It needs a removal tool with an address, not the row,
the page or Home Screen Sections. Here it is an action of the feature's one controller
(`JeMediaTagsController.LeavingSoon.cs`, `GetLeavingSoonItems`), taking its services as `[FromServices]` parameters
like the page's list route. The Leaving soon card gets upstream's "Copy KefinTweaks section" button; the engine
forwards a click on its Services tab copy to it, as upstream's does. `api.md` gains upstream's row (223 routes).

### 77–78. Hide watched titles in the home row

`LeavingSoonHideWatched` (off by default) leaves out of the Home Screen Sections row what Jellyfin counts as played for
the user. The toggle sits in the Leaving soon card after the copy button. Records: the What's New lexical index, the
admin-config baseline (two rendered ids, the schema id, the collected default), `config-flag-groups.json`, and the
production inventory (which is all upstream's entry 78 changes).

### 79. Seerr search keeps the region

Search sends the whole browser language tag (`pt-BR`, `zh-TW`), as upstream. This tree already sent English when the
browser language is not a language tag (Firefox without a locale reports `"undefined"`); that guard stays and accepts
the full tag shape, so upstream's raw `navigator.language` (which now sends `language=undefined` on such a Firefox) is
not copied.

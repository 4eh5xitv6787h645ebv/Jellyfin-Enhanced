  `discovery-filter-utils.ts` bundles them and publishes `JE.discoveryStyles`, `JE.discoveryBusy`,
  `JE.discoveryMenu`, `JE.discoveryRoles`, `JE.discoverySection` and `JE.discoveryFilters` in upstream's order,

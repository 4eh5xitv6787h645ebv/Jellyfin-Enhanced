## Flakes and product bugs

None were fixed. These are two test-harness timing flakes, not product bugs:
1. `tests/frontend/core/core-api-runtime.test.mjs:284`, "retry jitter and clamping…". The test records every `window.setTimeout` delay under 1000 ms and expects `[7, 14, 14]`. `js/core/navigation.js:131` polls `installEmbyHook` with `setTimeout(…, 100)`. When the retry loop takes longer than 100 ms of wall time, an extra `100` shows up (e.g. `[7, 14, 100, 14]`). Repro: under a load average of about 20, `node --test --test-name-pattern='retry jitter' tests/frontend/core/core-api-runtime.test.mjs` failed 7/10 on the Part 2 tree and 10/10 on `77e6075a` with Part 2 stashed. It passes on an idle machine and passed in both green gates. Candidate fix (not made): filter to the retry timer, or stub the navigation poll.

Playback & Tab Switch
Pause playback automatically when you switch to another browser tab.
Resume playback automatically when you switch back to the player tab.
Enter the browser's Picture-in-Picture mode when switching tabs (browser support required).
Long-press anywhere on the player to temporarily play at 2× speed; release to return to normal speed. Beta, touch devices only.
Briefly shows the age rating in a corner of the player when a video starts, followed by a line of any content-advisory tags (such as violence or strong language) and a line of its genres.
